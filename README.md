# SolidWorks Guitar Replica

<h2>Description</h2>
The goal of this project was to 3D-model an exact replica of my Schecter Omen Extreme 6 electric guitar. Throughout the modelling process I used calipers to precisely measure every feature of my guitar to make my model as accurate as possible. Some things not included in my model were the strings, audio jack, fretboard designs, as well as the color, which was very difficult to match. 
 
<h2>Mass Properties Comparison</h2>
The body and neck of the guitar are made of mahogany wood. I chose PF plastic for the pickups and switch, Plain Carbon Steel for the knobs and bridge, and 1060 Aluminum Alloy for the tuners. I weighed my guitar with a bathroom scale and found the weight to be ≈6.60 lbs. Analysis of my assembly showed it weighed 6.94 lbs.
